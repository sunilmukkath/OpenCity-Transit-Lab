Data Sources & Methodology

Census 2011

Government of India Census conducted in 2011

Coverage: 155 wards across Greater Chennai

Variables: Population, literacy, housing, occupation, sanitation

Access: OpenCity CKAN

Economic Census 2012-13

Ministry of Statistics, Government of India

Coverage: 3.33L businesses, 7.79L workers

Variables: Employment, business types, gender distribution, sector breakdown

Access: OpenCity CKAN

Slum Census Data

Census of India 2001 & 2011 Provisional Data

Coverage: 400+ slum settlements, 1.34M residents

Variables: Slum locations, population, housing type, basic amenities

Access: data.gov.in

OpenStreetMap

Collaborative global geographic database

Coverage: Complete Chennai metropolitan area

Variables: Bus routes, MRTS stations, metro lines, street networks, POIs

Access: OpenStreetMap.org

Transit Authority Data

MTC, CMRL, MRTS official sources

MTC: 668 bus routes, 3,488 buses, 3.233M daily passengers

MRTS: 21 stations, 23.98 km, 200k daily riders

Metro: 5 lines (Phase 1-4)

OpenCity CKAN Portal

Chennai's open government data portal

Datasets: Ward boundaries, demographic data, municipal records

License: Open Data Commons

Access: data.opencity.in

Methodology & Metrics

SEC Classification

Socioeconomic Classification (SEC) based on occupation and education of household head. 5-tier system: A/B (high), C (middle), D/E (low). Derived from Census 2011 proxy variables.

Transit Accessibility

% of population within 400m walking distance of nearest transit stop. Calculated using Overpass API for OSM data. Standard benchmark for urban transit accessibility.

Equity Score

Composite index (0-100) combining: Transit Access (35%), Infrastructure Quality (25%), Socioeconomic Vulnerability (25%), Employment Connectivity (15%).

Data Quality

Census 2011 is most recent comprehensive dataset. OSM data validated against official MTC/CMRL sources. Slum data from multiple years reconciled using spatial methods.

Limitations

Census 2011 is 15 years old. Ward boundaries changed 2015; data estimated using spatial allocation. Pedestrian infrastructure not in datasets; requires field audit.
